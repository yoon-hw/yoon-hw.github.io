Hyewon (Yuna) Yoon

Ph.D. Student, Urban and Regional Science, Texas A&M University

I am a Ph.D. student in Urban and Regional Science at Texas A&M University, My research sits at the intersection of environmental health, the built environment, and urban computing. I study how disasters and environmental hazards reshape neighborhoods, and what those changes mean for public health. My work combines Generative AI, computer vision, street-level imagery, and geospatial data science to measure built-environment conditions after disasters.
