Hyewon (Yuna) Yoon

Ph.D. Student, Urban and Regional Science, Texas A&M University

I am a Ph.D. student in Urban and Regional Science at Texas A&M University. My research sits at the intersection of environmental health, the built environment, and urban computing. I study how disasters and environmental hazards reshape neighborhoods, and what those changes mean for public health. My work combines generative AI, computer vision, street-level imagery, and geospatial data science to measure built-environment conditions after disasters.

Research

Neighborhood Physical Disorder after Hurricanes

A two-step deep learning pipeline detects physical disorder from Google Street View imagery, tracking how neighborhood conditions in Houston shifted before and after Hurricane Harvey.

Redlining and Hospital Utilization

Linking historical redlining grades and the Social Vulnerability Index to cancer-related hospital utilization across Texas, examining how past housing policy shapes present-day health.

Park Access and Green Exposure

Using smartphone mobility data and street-level imagery to reveal how highways act as barriers to park visitation and how green exposure is distributed across neighborhoods.
